Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how NaijaFootyBot ("the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our automated video creation and publishing tool.

2. Information We Collect

The App uses the TikTok Content Posting API to publish videos to the operator's own TikTok account. To do this, we access the following information:

  • Basic Profile Information: Your TikTok username and profile information, obtained via the user.info.basic scope. This is used solely to authenticate and identify the account for publishing.
  • Video Content: The videos generated by the App are uploaded to your TikTok account. We do not store these videos on external servers after publication.

The App does not collect personal data from third-party users or viewers of the published content.

3. How We Use Information

The information accessed is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate the operator's TikTok account.
  • Upload and publish AI-generated football prediction videos to that account.

We do not sell, rent, or share any information with third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

API access tokens are stored securely in a local environment file (.env) on the operator's own computer. We do not store any TikTok user data on external servers. The App runs locally and does not maintain a database of user information.

5. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with the following third-party services to function:

  • DeepSeek API: Used for generating video scripts. No personal user data is shared with DeepSeek.
  • Football-Data.org API: Used for retrieving match information. No personal user data is shared.
  • TikTok Content Posting API: Used for publishing videos to TikTok. Data handling is governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy.

6. Your Rights

As the operator of the App, you can revoke the App's access to your TikTok account at any time through your TikTok settings. You can also delete the stored access tokens from the .env file to stop all App activity.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
